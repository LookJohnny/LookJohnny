## Research focus

| Direction | What I am working on |
| --- | --- |
| **Emotion and behavioral-risk monitoring** | Investigating signals in supported model states and visible responses that can help identify concerning tone, hostility, excessive agreement, or possible fabrication. |
| **Agent safety controls** | Developing hooks, policy checks, sensitive-data handling, and human approval paths for supported agent workflows. |
| **Readable AI and human oversight** | Turning observations and safety decisions into readable signals, relevant excerpts, and evidence that people can inspect. |

I am interested in both the usefulness and the limits of these signals: what can actually be observed, when a warning is warranted, and how it should be presented to the person using the AI.

## Main project — Nous Agent Safety

**[Nous](https://github.com/LookJohnny/nous-agent-safety)** is my current Agent safety project. Its core focus is to:

- **Monitor** emotion-related and behavioral-risk signals in supported models and interactions.
- **Protect** supported workflows with safety checks, policy controls, and human intervention.
- **Make behavior readable** through risk feedback, contextual evidence, and interfaces for oversight.

Trajectory collection, review, and training JSONL export are **supporting capabilities** for auditing and further development.

The broader system remains in research and development. The public repository currently provides the supporting trajectory and data-review component; the full monitoring, protection, and interface prototypes are not yet included in that snapshot. Scope and limitations are documented in the [project README](https://github.com/LookJohnny/nous-agent-safety#components-and-availability) and its [中文版本](https://github.com/LookJohnny/nous-agent-safety/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md).
